# Lectures

The video series will be an *ordering* over the system pages, which are
the stable content. The order is drafted in pass 3 — each part's landing
page lists its lectures in watching order, and this page assembles them
with the dependencies between parts stated — and confirmed by the owner in
pass 6, after reading every part with the decompile open. Until then the
parts in the sidebar are the reading order: each part only assumes the
ones before it.
